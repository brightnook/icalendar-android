# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Effective date:** 2026-08-24

iOSCalendar is an Android calendar application and home-screen widget that synchronizes calendar information from iCloud Calendar. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information the app handles

When you choose to sign in, the app handles:

- Your Apple Account identifier and Apple Account app-specific password.
- iCloud calendar information needed to display your calendars and events, such as calendar names, event titles, dates, times, locations, notes, and event identifiers.
- App preferences, including language, synchronization interval, selected calendars, and theme settings.
- An optional background image that you import into the app.

Do not enter your Apple Account primary password. Use an app-specific password generated in your Apple Account security settings.

## How information is used

The app uses this information only to authenticate with iCloud, download and display calendar data, perform the synchronization interval you choose, and apply your local preferences. The app is read-only and does not create, edit, or delete iCloud calendar events.

Synchronization is performed directly between your device and Apple's iCloud service. The developer does not operate a separate server for calendar data, credentials, analytics, or advertising.

## Local storage and security

Calendar data, preferences, and optional theme images are stored in the app's private local storage. Your Apple Account identifier and app-specific password are encrypted before storage using a key held in Android Keystore.

You can sign out to remove locally stored sign-in credentials. You can also use the app's clear-cache option to remove locally cached calendar data. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data from the device.

## Sharing

The app does not sell your information and does not share it with advertising or analytics providers. Information required for synchronization is sent to Apple/iCloud so that the service can authenticate your account and return the calendars you requested.

## Your choices

You can choose manual synchronization, hourly synchronization, daily synchronization, or no background synchronization. You can sign out, clear cached data, or stop using the app at any time.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date before the related app release.
